You should play by the rules that disallow cheating AI:
* No encampments.
* Max 4 units of foot archers, as that composition is unrealiastic,
  allows to run around the battlefield. Horse archers are allowed in 
  unlimited number as that's what nomads were using.
* During minor and major settlement sieges, allowing the enemy to 
  take the main checkpoint means defeat.

Do not fall for special challenges in the game, aka, game achivements.
They are not fun. For example: `This is Total War` archivement means 
war with everyone from turn one. That will bring countless stacks of
enemy forces to fight off. Ideally we want to fight less.
